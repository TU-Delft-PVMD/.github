# Welcome to PVMD

We are the Photovoltaic Materials and Devices group at Delft University of Technology. Here we share research software developed within our group.

## PVMD Toolbox

A comprehensive modelling framework for simulating the performance and energy yield of photovoltaic systems, available through MATLAB and a graphical user interface.

**[Explore the PVMD Toolbox](https://github.com/TU-Delft-PVMD/PVMD_Toolbox)**

The repository includes the software, manuals, video tutorials and related publications.

## Maintenance and contributions

This organization is managed by the PVMD group at TU Delft. We welcome contributions from current and former group members and external collaborators.

For questions or bug reports, use the toolbox repository's Issues tab. Maintainer contact details are available in its README.

[Visit the PVMD group website](https://pvmd.ewi.tudelft.nl/)
